# How to get the installable app file (APK) — no coding needed

This will take about 10 minutes, and everything happens in your web browser.
You will not write or edit any code.

## Step 1 — Unzip the download
Find the file you downloaded (`international-business-android.zip`),
right-click it, and choose **Extract All** (Windows) or just double-click
it (Mac). You'll get a folder called `international-business`.

## Step 2 — Create a free GitHub account
GitHub is a website that will build the app for you automatically, for
free. Go to **https://github.com/signup** and create an account (just an
email and password).

## Step 3 — Create a new "repository" (just means a project folder online)
1. Once logged in, click the **+** icon in the top-right corner → **New repository**.
2. Give it any name, for example `international-business-game`.
3. Leave everything else as-is and click the green **Create repository** button.

## Step 4 — Upload the project
1. On the new (empty) repository page, click the link that says
   **"uploading an existing file"**.
2. Open the `international-business` folder you unzipped in Step 1, select
   **everything inside it** (all files and folders), and drag them into
   the browser window.
   - Important: drag what's *inside* the folder, not the folder itself.
3. Scroll down and click the green **Commit changes** button.

## Step 5 — Let the robot build your app
1. Click the **Actions** tab near the top of the page.
2. You'll see a build running (a yellow dot that turns into a green
   checkmark ✅ when done). This takes about 3–5 minutes — you can
   just wait or check back later.
3. Once it has a green checkmark, click on it.
4. Scroll down to the **Artifacts** section at the bottom and click
   **International-Business-APK** to download it.
5. This downloads a zip file — unzip it (same as Step 1) and you'll find
   **app-debug.apk** inside. That's your app!

## Step 6 — Install it on your Android phone
1. Get `app-debug.apk` onto your phone — easiest ways: email it to
   yourself and open the email on your phone, or upload it to Google
   Drive and open it there on your phone.
2. Tap the file on your phone to install it.
3. Android will likely warn you about "installing from unknown sources"
   since this isn't from the Play Store — this is normal and expected for
   an app built this way. Tap **Settings** in that prompt, allow it, then
   go back and tap the file again to install.
4. Open the app and play!
